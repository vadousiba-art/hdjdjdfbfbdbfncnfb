# YORU Daily: 3 videos a day, phone only

**One topic, every day:** a 17-year-old building his own social media app in
public. Reels and TikTok push accounts whose topic is clear, and a daily
series gives people a reason to follow ("I want to see Day 2").

Every day has the same 3 slots. The format repeats, the content is new.

| Slot | Name | When to post | Length | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Day N** (build log) | morning | 20–25 s | The series spine: real number of users today, what you're building, a question for the comments |
| 2 | **One feature, 15 seconds** | afternoon | 12–18 s | One thing YORU does, shown on the phone. Built for sends |
| 3 | **My app roasts me** | night | 15–20 s | YORU's AI Menace roasts your post; you react. Funny, built for replays |

Same edit as Reel 1 (`EDIT-STYLE.md`): big words behind you, the phone
sliding in with real app screens, word captions, a whoosh on everything.
Each video ends with a line that's different every day, so there's no
repeated outro.

## How to film (phone only)

**The setup (same every day, 2 minutes):**
- Phone **sideways** (landscape), front camera, like last time.
- **Prop it up, don't hold it**: on a shelf, a stack of books or a cup,
  about **1 metre** away (an arm and a half), at your eye level. Last time
  your face filled the whole frame; from 1 m away there's room around you
  for the big words.
- Stand or sit with the **wall/room behind you**, a lamp or window **in
  front of you**, not behind.
- Record in a quiet room. Keep the phone's mic facing you.

**Every clip:**
- Hit record, wait 1 second, say the line, wait 1 second, stop.
- Say each line **2 times** (I pick the best). If you mess up a word, just
  start the line again in the same clip.
- Look at the lens, not at yourself on the screen.

**Screen recordings:** the phone's built-in screen recorder, 5–10 s each,
brightness all the way up, notifications off (Do Not Disturb).

**Daily routine (about 20 minutes):** film all 3 videos in one go
(slot 1, 2, 3 clips back to back) → send me everything with the real user
count → I edit all 3 → you post them morning, afternoon, night.

**Always real:** the user number in slot 1 is the real number from your
admin page, even if it's small. Small and honest grows; fake gets caught.
