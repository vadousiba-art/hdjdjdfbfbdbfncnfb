# How we edit our videos (house style)

Taken from the three reference videos Ethan sent on 2026-11-03: a seated
"this video was edited with AI" talking head, a walk-and-talk with big
kinetic text, and Ethan's own "Day 1" series video. Every edit from now on
follows this unless a brief says otherwise.

## Pace
- No pause longer than about 0.3 s. Cut the breaths, never clip a word.
- Change something every 2 to 4 s: a jump cut with a punch-in (100% → 115%),
  a new location, a B-roll insert, or a pop-up card.
- Walk-and-talk: same sentence, new spot. The speech keeps going over the
  location cut.
- The hook lands in the first 1.5 s. No "hey guys", no intro.

## Captions (two layers)
1. **Word captions**: 1 to 3 words at a time, lower middle, bold white sans
   with a soft shadow. One keyword per line gets the accent: italic serif or
   brand purple. Captions follow the words to the frame.
2. **Kinetic keywords**: the 3 to 6 words that matter (numbers, the promise,
   the twist) go BIG behind the person (subject cut out), 3D or gradient,
   then shrink or fly out. Examples from the references: "IN LESS THAN 10
   SECONDS", "DIDN'T HAPPEN BY ACCIDENT", "ATTENTION".

## Pop-ups (each one gets a whoosh or a soft click)
- App logos and icons popping beside the head when named.
- Phone mockups showing real screens, sliding in from the side.
- Counters that tick up (views, users, days). Only real numbers.
- Floating reactions (the reference uses hearts; YORU uses its own: 🤡 💀 🧢 W L).
- Checklist cards that tick off as he talks.
- A "stamp" card for contrast (the reference's "AI SLOP" stamp).
- Typing or chat-box cards when a prompt or message is mentioned.

## Look
- Real places and real rooms. Handheld is fine; it should feel filmed by
  him, not by an agency.
- Face bright and clean: skin-based white balance, light denoise, lifted
  exposure (reel/grade.py, reel/exposure.py).
- Our own handle is fine; never a TikTok or CapCut watermark (Instagram cuts
  reposts from cold reach).

## Sound
- Voice first: cleaned, about -14 LUFS master, music ducked about 16 dB
  under speech.
- An original score or licensed audio only. Whoosh and click on every
  pop-up, a riser into the payoff.

## Ending
- Not "follow for more" and not "thanks for watching". End on a send prompt
  naming one specific person ("send this to the friend who…"), in voice and
  on screen, then cut straight back to the first frame so it loops.

## Added 2026-11-04 (from the motion-graphics and balloon references)

**Motion graphics layer:**
- Words arrive one at a time: blurred and slightly large, then sharp.
- Fake app UI being used: a cursor, a search bar that types, a result card
  sliding up, a "+" that grows into a button and clicks to "✓".
- Glass cards (frosted, soft shadow) that pop in beside the speaker.
- Motion blur on every move; nothing just appears.

**The hook formula (balloon):**
1. Open on an event that hasn't happened yet: something about to break,
   pop or land, with a countdown.
2. Count the viewer's own watch time on screen.
3. At about 7 s, call it out: "You just watched ___ for 7 seconds… and
   you're still here."
4. Turn it to YORU.
5. Pay off the event only at the very end, then loop to the first frame.
