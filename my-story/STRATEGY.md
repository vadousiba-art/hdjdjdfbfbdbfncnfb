# YORU content strategy: what's working right now, and how we make it

Researched 2026-11-04. **Honest limits:** I can't open Instagram or see real
view counts. This comes from this month's public trend reports (mostly
marketing blogs, so directional, not lab data), what Instagram has said
about ranking, and the reference videos you sent me. Sources at the end.

---

## 1. What's working in your niche right now

**What Instagram rewards (the January 2025 ranking statement, still the
basis everyone quotes):**
- **Watch time** (people finishing it): the strongest single signal.
- **Sends per reach** (people DM-ing it): the biggest one for reaching
  people who don't follow you. This is the one that makes a video go
  viral.
- **Likes per reach**: matters most for your existing followers.
- **Originality**: reposts and recycled clips get pushed down.

**Formats breaking out for founders (weekly founder-video report, Oct
2026):**

| Format | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Public failure and fix, as a series** ("Day 1… it broke, here's the fix, you pick the next step") | **strongest** | Unresolved tension, and viewers take part |
| **"I hated this, so I built that"** (the founder's own problem → the product) | strong | A story with a reason, then a fast demo |
| **Founder opinion with a target** ("X is manipulation") | strong | Tension and a clear stance |
| **Mistake → lesson**, specific and first person | stronger than generic motivation | Real and specific beats vague |
| Founder reacting to proof (a user, a mention) | good | Emotion only the founder has |
| **Generic day-in-the-life** | **weakest** | Routine has no suspense |
| **Generic inspiration** with no target or tension | weak | "Fails without a clear target, evidence or tension" |

Also this month:
- **Raw and real beats glossy.** Proof of work (the actual code, the
  actual bug) beats polish.
- **15–30 s** is still the most reliable length for people finishing it.
- **Captions** are expected, because most people watch on mute.
- The big trending sound right now is "Housewife" (Tate McRae), plus
  Christmas songs. That's not our lane; don't force it.

**The hook rules that keep showing up:**
- **Movement in the first frame.**
- **Text on screen in the first fraction of a second.**
- **The stake named by about the fifth word** ("I'm 17 and…" does it at
  word 3).
- **Never open with** "Are you tired of…", "Have you ever wondered…",
  "Hey guys", "So…".
- If fewer than about half the viewers get past the first 3 seconds, the
  video usually stalls around 200 views. The hook is the whole game.

### ⚠️ One thing that works against the 100k goal: landscape

A 16:9 video on Reels shows with black bars and fills only about a third
of the screen. Our landscape look is great, but **for Reels, post a 9:16
version**. From 1 metre away (like your last night video) a vertical crop
fits you well, and I can render both from the same footage: **landscape
for TikTok/YouTube and your archive, vertical for Reels.** Your style
stays exactly the same; it just fills the phone. I recommend this for
every video from now on. Tell me if you'd rather stay landscape-only.

## 2. Our four formats (rotate them, all in the locked style)

| # | Format | Built on | Posts / week |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **"Fix it with me"**: Day N of building YORU in public. Something broke or needs deciding; you show it, try one fix, and **viewers pick the next step** in the comments. | the strongest founder format | 3 |
| **B** | **3 a.m. thoughts**: one lesson from **your real story** (a specific mistake → what it taught you), quiet, the repeat device. | mistake → lesson, motivational | 2 |
| **C** | **POV**: the viewer's own night ("POV: it's 3am and you're still checking your likes"). | direct address with a stake | 1 |
| **D** | **"I hated this, so I built that"**: one YORU feature through the problem it fixes (clown reactions because likes feel fake, Ghost Mode because seen-at stresses you out). | founder-inventor demo | 1 |

They all end on 夜 · *the night is for everyone*. **A** gives people a
reason to follow (they want to see what you fix next). **B/C** get sends.
**D** turns viewers into users.

## 3. Hook bank (written for you, in the six patterns that work)

**Only say the true ones.** Swap in your real numbers.

**Cold open on a scene:**
- "2:14am. My app just broke for every single user."
- "11pm. Everyone's asleep. I'm still fixing the same bug."

**A number that needs explaining:**
- "I've spent [N] nights building one app."
- "My app has [N] users. I know every single one of their names."

**Direct address with a stake:**
- "If you're 17 and someone told you you're too young…"
- "If you're watching this at 3am, this is for you."

**Tease and reveal:**
- "There's one button on my app Instagram will never add."
- "I deleted the like button. Here's what happened."

**Contrarian:**
- "Likes are the worst thing that happened to social media."
- "You don't need more followers. You need [one thing]."

**List:**
- "3 things I learned building a social media app at 17."
- "3 bugs that almost killed my app this week."

## 4. The method (every video, the same steps)

1. **Pick the format** (A/B/C/D) and **one idea**: one feeling, one
   lesson, one fix.
2. **Write the hook first.** Check it against the rules above. Write **3
   versions** of the first line.
3. **Script in beats, not paragraphs:**
   hook (≤2 s) → stake/promise (by 3 s) → tension (the problem, the
   number, the unfinished thing) → the turn (what you did / what it
   means) → payoff (last) → one line that points to YORU or the series.
   15–30 s total.
4. **Record** (section 5 below): the setup from `YORU-STYLE.md`. 3 takes.
   Each hook version recorded separately.
5. **I edit** it in the locked style, and render **9:16 for Reels**
   (+16:9 if you want).
6. **Post** at 10 p.m.–midnight. Hook versions go up as **Trial Reels**;
   the winner after 24 h is posted to everyone.
7. **Read 3 numbers after 24 h:**
   - **3-second hold** (aim for 50% or more).
   - **Average watch time** (more than half the video).
   - **Shares per 1,000 views** (the viral one).

   Send me screenshots.
8. **Double down:** whatever wins, make 2 more of it within 48 h.
   Whatever loses twice, drop it.

## 5. How to talk in your videos

**1. The first word is the hook.** No "so", "hey guys", "um", or a
breath first. Start the sentence the instant the camera is rolling (I cut
the silence before it).

**2. Look at the lens, not at yourself.**
- Your eyes drift to your face on the screen, and that reads as looking
  away.
- Put a small sticker right next to the front camera and talk to it.
- Look away only on purpose, when you're "remembering" something, then
  come back to the lens for the key line.

**3. Pick the energy for the format.**
- **"Fix it with me" (A) and "I built that" (D):** about **20–30% more
  energy than feels normal.** Cameras flatten you, so what feels like a
  bit much looks normal. Slightly faster than you'd talk to a friend.
- **3 a.m. thoughts (B) and POV (C):** the opposite. **Slower and lower
  than normal**, like someone's asleep next door. Pauses are part of it.

**4. Talk to one person.** Picture one friend watching at night, and say
"you", not "you guys" or "everyone". The video is going to one phone at a
time.

**5. One thought per breath.**
- Short sentences.
- Breathe at the full stop, not in the middle of a line.
- If a line is too long to say in one breath, split it.

**6. Hit one word per line.** Every line has a word that matters ("*eleven*
times", "*at night*", "*nobody*"). Lean into it slightly: a little louder,
or a tiny pause before it. Those are the words I put behind you.

**7. Pause before the important line, not after.** Half a second of
silence before "Now let that sink in" makes people lean in. Dead air after
a line makes them swipe. (I trim the dead air; you give me the pauses
before the important lines.)

**8. Drop your voice at the end of the sentence.** Ending low sounds sure;
ending high sounds like a question and less confident. ("I built it at
night." down, not up.)

**9. Hands and face.**
- Hands low, near your chest, moving only to point or count.
- Don't fidget.
- On 3 a.m. videos, stay almost still.
- On energetic ones, smile with your eyes on the first line.

**10. Don't read it, know it.**
- Learn the **beats**, not the words.
- If you forget, say it your way. Your improvised lines in Reel 3 ("like
  a good little dog") were better than mine.

**11. Messed up? Keep rolling.** Pause, go back to the start of that
sentence, and say it again. I'll cut it. Never stop the take for a stumble.

**12. A 2-minute daily drill.** Every day, record a 20-second answer to
"what did you build today?" to the lens, then watch it back once. Note one
thing (eyes, speed, energy) and fix only that tomorrow. In two weeks you'll
look like a different person on camera.
