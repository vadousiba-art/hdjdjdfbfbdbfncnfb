# YORU video: "Day in the life: 17, in school, building a social media app"

Tomorrow's video (Nov 6), and Day 1 of the 1,000-people goal (`YORU-GOAL-1.md`). Edited
with the locked v2 rules (`YORU-STYLE.md`) and the Brand Lab look (`reels/brand/BRAND.md`).

**The idea:** student by day, founder by night. The day is quick, quiet, normal school
clips. Then the lights go off and the real job starts, which is YORU's whole brand (夜 =
night). It ends on the counter: Day 1, [N] / 1,000.

**Only keep what's true.** The times, the classes, what you do at lunch, what you built
that night: swap in your real day. If a line isn't true for you, cut it or tell me and I'll
rewrite it.

## Hook (pick one; the other two become test versions)

## How to record it (phone only, vertical)

- **Every clip vertical**, 3-5 seconds long (I cut each to 1-3 s). Hold the phone still or
  move slowly. Film each B-roll clip twice.
- **Day clips:** natural light, face the window. **Night clips:** room dark, lamp or screen on.
  Same hoodie and sunglasses as the goal video, so it reads like the same series.
- **The voiceover:** record it tonight or tomorrow night as a normal video of you talking to
  the camera at your desk (quiet room, phone close). Read each line 2 times. I use the audio
  over the day clips, and scene 11 straight from it.
- **At school:** film only where it's allowed, and keep classmates' and teachers' faces out
  of the shot (no one in frame who didn't say yes). Empty hallway, your locker, your desk,
  your notebook, the building from outside: those all work.
- **Scene 6 (DMs):** film your phone from over your shoulder. I'll blur the usernames.
- **Scene 10:** whatever you really worked on, even a small fix. If you'd rather, tell me what
  it was and I'll capture that screen from the app.

## Why it can work

"Day in the life" is a format people already watch to the end, and "17 and in school" makes
it surprising. The day/night split is YORU's brand without having to say it, and ending on
the counter makes it the next episode of the goal, so people who saw Day 0 come back.
