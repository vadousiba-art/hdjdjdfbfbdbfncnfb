# YORU video: "Can a 17 y/o get 1,000 people on his own app?"

Modelled on the "can a 15 y/o go from 2K to 10K followers in 30 days" video, edited with
the locked v2 rules (`YORU-STYLE.md`) and the approved Brand Lab look (`reels/brand/BRAND.md`).

## What we keep from the reference, and what changes

| The reference | Ours |
| --- | --- |
| A public goal with a deadline: followers, 2K to 10K in 30 days | The goal is **people on YORU**, so the video grows the app, not just the account |
| Proof of the starting point: his Instagram profile card | A YORU-style counter card with your **real** user count and a goal bar |
| He raises his goal mid-video ("I can do even better") | Cut. We don't claim a growth rate we can't show |
| "30 days. Wait... oh, 28 days" panic beat | Same beat, with the real days left in November |
| A walking shot and laptop shots as cutaways | Same: one walking shot, hands typing at night |
| Ends on "drop a follow" and an Instagram end card | Ends on the keyword CTA, **no end card** (rule 8) |
| Serif captions, moving Instagram handle | Red-keyword Inter captions, the handle fixed in the corner |
| Shot landscape | **Vertical** |

Every number in it must be true on the day you record: the user count, the days left.

## How to record it (phone only)

- **Vertical.** Phone propped at eye level, about an arm's length away, the lamp or window in
  front of you, not behind. Same outfit, same spot for every desk scene.
- Hold something as a mic if you like (the reference holds one); it gives your hands a job.
- **Record each scene as its own clip, in order: S1, S2, S3, B1, S4, S5, S6, S7.** Do each
  2-3 times. Leave a second of silence before and after you talk; I cut every pause.
- Talk to the lens, a bit faster than normal, with energy. S2 and S6 are reactions: make them big.
- **S3, the walking shot:** outside at dusk, phone held selfie-style at arm's length, walking
  slowly toward the camera while you talk. 2 takes.
- **B1:** at your desk with the room dark and the screen lit: phone on the desk pointing at
  your hands and the keyboard, 5-10 s of typing.

## How I'll edit it

- Plain cuts, every pause over 0.1 s gone, your voice cleaned to -14 LUFS, no music
  (add a trending sound in Instagram).
- Hook in white boxes on S1. Captions in red-keyword style the whole way.
- Inserts: the real YORU app in the split screen (I capture it), the counter card and goal bar
  in the app-card style, the 30 → [days] countdown, and the NIGHT CTA card. All approved in
  the Brand Lab.
- Your handle in the corner, the whole video.

## Why it can work

A goal with a deadline gives people a reason to come back and see if you made it, so every
follow-up ("Day 7: 140 / 1,000") is a sequel. Commenting NIGHT is the way to help you make it,
and every one of those comments is a real person you invite.
