# YORU video: Day 2 of "1,000 people on my app"

The follow-up to `YORU-GOAL-1.md` ("Can a 17 y/o get 1,000 people on his own app in 30
days?"). Same setup, same look, so it reads as the next episode. Edited with the locked v2
rules and the Brand Lab look.

**The idea:** people who saw Day 0 come back for one thing: did the number move? So we
answer it fast, show the proof, say what's next, and ask again.

**Every number must be real on the day you post:** the user count (Admin → Members), the
views and comments on the goal video, the days left (to Nov 30).

## Hook (pick one; the others become test versions)

- A, the reveal: **Day 2: did anyone actually join my app?** (white boxes)
- B, the stakes: **Day 2: [N] / 1,000 people. [days] days left.** (black boxes)
- C, the reaction: open on your reaction to the number, no text for the first second, then A.

## The script (about 30 s)

| # | Where | You say | On screen (I add it) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Two ways S2 can go, both work:**
- **It moved:** celebrate for real. "[N]! Let's go." Then S3.
- **It barely moved / didn't:** deadpan. "Fourteen. Still... fourteen." That's honest and funny,
  and it makes people want to be the one who moves it. Then go straight to S4 and skip the
  "sent every one of you the link" line if nobody commented.

**Keep the swears if they come out.** Same as last time, I leave them in.

  and after. React big on S2 and S4.
