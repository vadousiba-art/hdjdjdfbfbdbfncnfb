## Video 2: "my reaction to…", the countdown reaction trend

**Format of the reference:**
- **The setup:** 11 seconds, no talking, sitting at your desk (wide shot).
  Top text `my reaction to the age you start a business`, and a big number
  under it counting **down** (40 → 13).
- **The reaction:** you react to each number. Disgust and a punch at 40,
  a shrug at the late 20s, a nod around 20, thumbs up in the teens. It
  ends on a smile at the youngest age.
- **Why it works:** it's very short, it loops, and people comment their
  age.

**Option A (closest to the reference):** `my reaction to the age you build your first app`
- The numbers count down **40 → 17**, and it ends on 17 (your age) with you
  pointing at yourself and grinning.

**Option B (YORU's message):** `my reaction to how many likes you need to feel good about a post`
- The numbers count down **10,000 → 1**, from disgust to approval. It ends
  on **1**: you nod, a big smile, a thumbs up.

| Number on screen | Your reaction (no talking) |
|---|---|
| 40 / 10,000 | disgusted face, fake punch at the camera |
| 36–30 / 5,000–1,000 | shake your head, "no" with your finger |
| 29–23 / 500–100 | shrug, "meh" with your hand |
| 22–19 / 50–20 | slow nod, thinking |
| 18–17 / 10–1 | thumbs up, then two thumbs up and a big smile |

**Record:**
- Phone **vertical, propped up about 1.5 m away** so we see you sitting at
  your desk from the waist up.
- Play the reactions in order, **one every 0.6–0.8 s**, about **11 s**
  total, with no talking.
- **2–3 takes.**
- I add the top text and the counting number (white, plain, no
  background), cut to the beat.
- **You add a trending sound inside Instagram** when you post (licensed
  through the app).
