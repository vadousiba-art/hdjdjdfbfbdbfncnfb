# YORU Night Thoughts: the 3 a.m. videos

## What the two references do

**"Dreams come true when you don't sleep" (9.5 s).** Dark room, face close
to the camera, said quietly. One line, then "Think about it.", then the
**same line again** with the key words in big capitals (DREAMS · COME ·
SLEEP), then "Now let that sink in." and a fade to black. It's short, so
people watch it twice and it loops.

**"If you knew you were 50 failures away…" (48 s).** Same dark room, a
question asked slowly, then a **few seconds of pure black** before the
next one. The second question **raises the number** (50 → 100). Then a
short command ("Start learning to fail.") and an answer line. Captions
type in **letter by letter**, small, monospace, left-aligned, like a
journal.

**Why they hook:**
- **It feels private.** Night, dark, a quiet voice: it feels like a thought
  you weren't supposed to hear.
- **It asks something.** A question makes the viewer answer it in their
  head, so they stay.
- **The repeat.** Saying the line twice makes it stick, and people share
  lines that stick.
- **The silence.** The black pauses make you lean in. (We keep ours short,
  about 1.5 s; longer and people swipe.)

**Why it's perfect for YORU:** YORU means night. This becomes our own
series, **"3 a.m. thoughts"**, the brand's night voice. No feature pitch, just
a feeling people send to friends, ending on 夜 YORU.

## How to record (phone only)

- **At night, for real.** Room lights off. **One** soft light on your face
  from the side: a lamp turned away from you, your laptop screen, or your
  phone's flashlight bounced off a wall. The rest of the room should be
  dark. That's the whole look.
- **This one is vertical** (phone upright). These videos are close and
  personal, and vertical fills the screen with your face. Front camera,
  phone propped **about half a metre** away at eye level (closer than
  before), or hold it like a FaceTime call.
- **Speak quietly and slowly**, like it's 3 a.m. and someone's asleep in the
  next room. Leave a **2-second pause** between lines. Look into the lens.
  Don't smile; be calm.
- **Record each video in one take**, all its lines, start to finish. Do it
  **3 times**. I'll pick the best one, or mix lines from different takes.
- Wear something plain and dark if you can. Quiet room: no fan, no TV.

**Send me:** the 3 takes of each video you want made (Video 1, 2, 3, or
just Video 1).

## The edit (I do this)

- Darker grade with a slight warm-red tint (YORU's night look), soft grain,
  light vignette, a slow push-in on your face.
- Captions type in letter by letter (small monospace, like the reference),
  with the key lines in big capitals.
- Black pauses of about 1.5 s between thoughts, with only a small 夜 in the
  corner.
- A very quiet low drone and the room's own silence. No beat.
- The end: fade to black, 夜 YORU, your handle. The last frame matches
  the first, so it loops.
