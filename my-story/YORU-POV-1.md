# YORU POV: "it's 3 a.m. and you're still checking your likes"

## The format

**The edit from the reference** ("Dreams come true when you don't sleep"):
dark room, close, quiet. One line. "Think about it." The **same line
again**, slower, with the key words big. "Now let that sink in." Fade to
black. About 10–15 s, so people watch it twice.

**Plus a POV hook:**
- The very first frame says what the viewer is doing **right now**:
  "POV: it's 3am and you're still checking your likes". Half the people
  watching at night are doing exactly that, so they feel caught and stay.
- **The move:** you start looking down at a screen, lit by it, like you're
  scrolling too. On the first line you **look up into the lens**. That
  change grabs attention, and it feels like you're talking to them.

It's the same series as "3 a.m. thoughts" (same night look, words behind
you, typewriter captions, 夜 at the end), so it builds the brand. And it's
quietly YORU's whole point (likes that don't mean anything), without
selling.

## How to record (phone only)

- **Phone sideways (landscape)**, front camera, propped up about **1 metre
  away** at eye level, like the last one, so there's room for the words
  behind you.
- **Night, room lights off.** The only light is your **laptop screen in
  front of you, just below your face**, so you're lit like someone
  scrolling at 3 a.m. (Turn its brightness all the way up and open
  something white.)
- **The opening:** start each take **looking down at the laptop**, scroll
  with your finger for **2 seconds** without talking, **then look up into
  the phone's lens** and start line 2. That look-up is the hook.
- **Quiet and slow**, like someone's asleep next door. **2-second pause**
  between lines. On "Think about it." stay still and hold eye contact.
- Say the repeated line **slower** the second time, with a little pause
  in the middle ("Eleven times… for people you don't even talk to").
- **One take per video, start to finish, 3 times.** Quiet room (no fan,
  no TV).
