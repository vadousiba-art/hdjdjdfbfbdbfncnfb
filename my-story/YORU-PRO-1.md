# YORU video: "I'm 17 and I just asked the internet for money"

The episode where YORU Pro opens. It's the same series as the 1,000-people goal videos (Day 0,
Day 2), with the same desk, hoodie and sunglasses. This time the news is: **you can pay for
Pro now, or donate**, and a real thank-you to everyone who's shown up so far.

**Why it can travel:** the way people pay is funny on its own. It's an e-Transfer, like paying
your roommate back, and the cents are how you know who paid. Strangers will want to explain
that to a friend. The thank-you gives it heart, and the last line ("...but pay me") flips back
to the joke so it loops.

**Everything said must be true on the day you post.** Facts checked on Nov 5:
- Pro is **CA$5.99 for one month**, paid by **Interac e-Transfer**. **Nothing renews.** Each
  checkout gives an exact amount a few cents under $5.99 plus a reference code.
- Donations are **$2 to $500**. They're a thank-you and don't buy anything.
- Posting, chatting, privacy tools and 115 cosmetics stay free. Pro never changes who sees
  your posts.
- Every day you type in what arrived, and the app shows who paid and who didn't.
- Members: **14 on Day 0, 27 right now** (Admin → Members). Use the number on the day you post.
- **Don't mention PayPal or cards at all** (series rule).

## How to record (same as the goal videos)

- **Vertical**, phone propped at eye level, arm's length, same desk, same lamp, **same hoodie
  and sunglasses** (they stay on, every take).
- Each scene is its own clip, in order (S1–S8, then the extras). Do 2–3 takes each, with a
  second of silence before and after.
- **Play the jokes straight.** Deadpan on S1, S3 and S4 is what makes them land. On S4, lean
  in on "...and who didn't." Then **change gear on S6:** slower, quieter, no joke. The
  contrast is the point. Then the little smile on "...But pay me."
