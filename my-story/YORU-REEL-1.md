# YORU Reel 1: "4 things my app does that Instagram never will"

**One line:** a 17-year-old walking at night, showing the social media app he
built on his own phone, listing 4 things it does that Instagram won't.

- **Length:** 28 s, 9:16, 1080×1920. Same file for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.
- **Topic, kept clear on purpose:** "teen builds his own social media app".
  Every word on screen says that, so Instagram and TikTok know who to show it to.
- **Built on the skills' rules:**
  - Something moves in the first 1.3 s.
  - Instagram checks in at 3, 8 and 12 s: the promise by 3 s, the first
    payoff by 8 s, the second by 12 s.
  - It ends on a send prompt to one specific person (DM shares are the
    strongest signal on Reels).
  - The last frame cuts straight back to the first, so it loops.
- **Edit style:** `EDIT-STYLE.md` (big words behind you, 1–3 word captions,
  pop-ups with a whoosh, a cut every 2–4 s).

## The clips to record

Record each clip as its own short video. Do every talking clip **3 times**.
Leave 1 second of silence before you start and after you stop. If you trip
over a word, just say the line again; I'll pick the best take.

**Where and how (all talking clips, "A"):** at night, walking slowly toward
the camera on one street, moving to a new spot for each clip (storefront,
streetlight, crosswalk, parking lot). Phone vertical at eye level, arm's
length or held by a friend. **Your face has to be bright:** light falling
on your face, never behind you (lit store window, streetlight above, or a
cheap clip-on light). Mic close to your mouth.

### Talking clips

**CLIP 1: the hook (record it 5 times).**
Start with the phone screen pressed against the camera lens, YORU open on
a post covered in 🤡. Pull the phone away in one move and say:
> "I'm seventeen, and I built my own social media app."

Try 5 energies: excited / calm / proud / almost whispering / laughing.

**CLIP 2: the promise.** New spot, still walking.
> "Here are four things it does that Instagram never will."

**CLIP 3: one.** Hold your phone up beside your face.
> "One: you don't have to heart a post. You can clown it."

**CLIP 4: two.**
> "Two: Ghost Mode. Nobody sees when you're online. Ever."

**CLIP 5: three.** (Check that AI roasts work on your live account first.)
> "Three: if your friends let it, an AI roasts their posts."

If roasts don't work yet, record this instead:
> "Three: four hundred ways to make your profile look like you."

**CLIP 6: four.** Stop walking. Closer to the camera, serious, slower.
> "And four: paying never gets you more views. Everyone gets the same reach."

**CLIP 7: the real one.** At your desk at night, room lights off, face lit
only by the laptop with VS Code open. Camera at your side (profile view).
Type for 3 seconds, then turn your head to the camera and say:
> "I built it at night. That's literally what YORU means."

**CLIP 8: the ending.** Back outside, walking toward the camera.
> "Send this to the friend who'd clown every one of your posts."

Then laugh, keep walking, and keep filming for 2 more seconds.

**CLIP 9: full run (2 takes).** The whole script in one go while you walk,
start to finish. It's a backup in case the separate clips feel stiff.

### Screen and B-roll clips (no talking)

**CLIP 11: screen recording, reactions (10 s).** Your phone's built-in screen

**CLIP 14: screen recording, the hook post (5 s).** The post covered in 🤡

**CLIP 15: laptop close-up (5 s).** Just your hands typing in VS Code, night.

**CLIP 16 (optional, 3 s).** A YORU notification arriving on your lock screen.
