# YORU Reel 1: "4 things my app does that Instagram never will"

**Goal:** cold reach and app sign-ups. **Length:** about 28 s, 9:16.
**Structure:** a list with a memorable name, closed by a send prompt to a
named person (from the `viral-instagram-reels` skill: sends to non-followers
count for more than likes). **Style:** `EDIT-STYLE.md`.
**Why it can feel real:** a 17-year-old, at night, showing his own app on
his own phone. Nothing scripted that isn't true.

## Script

| Time | He says | Big text / pop-up | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 | "I'm seventeen, and I built my own social media app." | **I'M 17** (huge, behind him) → **I BUILT MY OWN APP** | A1: walking toward camera at night, holding the phone up to the lens with YORU open, then pulling it away to show his face |
| 2.5–5.0 | "Here are four things it does that Instagram never will." | **4 THINGS INSTAGRAM WON'T DO**, with a 1/4 counter card | A1, new spot |
| 5.0–9.5 | "One: you don't have to heart a post. You can clown it." | **1. 🤡 > ❤️**, floating clowns and skulls instead of hearts | B1: his thumb putting a 🤡 on a post, then 💀 🧢 W |
| 9.5–13.5 | "Two: Ghost Mode. Nobody sees when you're online. Ever." | **2. GHOST MODE**, a green online dot turning off | B2: screen recording of the Ghost Mode switch |
| 13.5–18.0 | "Three: if your friends let it, an AI roasts their posts." | **3. AI ROASTS**, roast card slides in | B2: a Menace roast appearing under a post |
| 18.0–22.5 | "And four: paying never gets you more views. Everyone gets the same reach." | **4. NO PAY-TO-WIN**, a stamp | A1, closer, serious beat |
| 22.5–25.5 | "I built it at night. That's literally what YORU means." | **夜 = NIGHT** | B3: his face lit by VS Code at 2 a.m., then the YORU logo |
| 25.5–28.5 | "Send this to the friend who'd clown every one of your posts." | **send this to the friend who'd 🤡 your posts** | A1: he laughs, then a hard cut back to frame one (loop) |

Say it as if you're telling a friend, not reading. If a word comes out a
little wrong, keep going; I'll cut it.

**Check before posting:** make sure the AI roast (line 3) works on your live
account. If it doesn't, swap in: "Three: four hundred ways to make your
profile look like you."

## Shots to record

**A1, talking (main shots).** 3 full takes of the whole script, each in a
different spot along one walk (street, storefront, parking lot). Phone at
eye level, arm's length or on a stick, filmed vertically, 1080p or 4K at 30
fps, back camera if someone can hold it.
- Night, but your face has to be **bright**: stand under a streetlight or
  in front of a lit store window, light on your face, not behind you. (Last
  time the face came out dark and grainy; a $15 clip-on phone light fixes it.)
- Walk slowly toward the camera while you talk. Stop for the "four: paying
  never…" line.
- Record the hook (0–2.5 s) 5 extra times with different energy: excited,
  calm, almost whispering. We'll pick the best.
- Leave 1 s of silence before and after each take.

**B1, over the shoulder.** Camera behind your shoulder, filming your phone
screen while you clown, skull and cap a post. 10 s. Screen brightness all
the way up.

**B2, screen recordings (phone's built-in recorder).**
- Putting reactions on a post (🤡 💀 🧢 W L): 10 s
- Settings → Ghost Mode switch, on and off: 5 s
- A post getting a Menace roast: 10 s
- Changing your profile ring or banner (backup for line 3): 10 s

**B3, the real one.** Side angle of you at the laptop at night, VS Code
open, face lit only by the screen. Just typing, 10 s. Plus 3 s of hands on
the keyboard.

**B4, extras (optional).** A YORU notification arriving on your lock screen;
you walking away from the camera at the end of the street.

**Sound.** Mic close to your mouth (a lav, or AirPods if that's all you
have), away from traffic. Send the audio as separate files if you use a mic
app.

**Send me:** the A1 takes, B1, the B2 recordings and B3. Name them or just
tell me which is which; I'll match them.
