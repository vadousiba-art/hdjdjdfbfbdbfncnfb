# YORU Reel 2: "Post on my app and I'll put you in my next video"

**Goal: sign-ups.** Reel 1 showed what YORU does. This one gives people a
reason to join **today**: get your post roasted by YORU's AI, on video, in
front of everyone who watches. Every night's roast video then becomes a new
ad for the next round, so it keeps bringing people in.

- **Length:** about 25 s, landscape, same edit as Reel 1.
- **Why it can work:**
  - It asks for one small, specific action (join, post with #roastme)
    and pays it back right away (you might be in tomorrow's video).
  - It's honest that you need your first users, which makes people want to
    help.
  - Each roast video creates the next one, so the series feeds itself.
- **Consent is built in:** YORU only lets the AI roast someone's post if
  they've switched on **Settings → "Let others roast me"**. Only feature
  posts tagged **#roastme** from people who have it on.

## The script

| Clip | Say | On screen (I add) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "I'm seventeen, I built my own social media app, and I need my first hundred users." | big **FIRST 100**, a counter ticking 0 → 100 |
| 2 | "So here's the deal." | big **THE DEAL** |
| 3 | "Join YORU. It's free, link in my bio." | phone: the sign-up screen |
| 4 | "Post anything with hashtag roast me." | big **#ROASTME**, phone: a post being written with #roastme |
| 5 | "Every night I'll pick three posts and let my app's AI roast them, on video." | phone: a real roast, 🔥 burst |
| 6 | "If yours gets picked, you're in my next video." | stamp **YOU'RE IN THE NEXT VIDEO** |
| 7 | "Send this to the friend who'd get roasted the hardest." | send card, 🤡 burst |

**Optional line 6b** (only if you'll really do it; you can, from the admin
page, with "Grant Pro"):
> "And the first hundred people get Pro free for a month."

If you say it, you have to give it, or people will call it out in the
comments.

## What to record (phone only)

Same setup as YORU Daily: phone sideways, front camera, propped up about
**1 metre away** at eye level, light in front of you, quiet room. Wait 1 s,
say the line, wait 1 s. **Say each line 2 times.**

| Clip | How |
|---|---|
| 1 | Look straight in the lens, serious, a little tired. This one's real. |
| 2 | Lean in a bit, like you're telling a secret. |
| 3 | Point to the side (where the phone screen will slide in). |
| 4 | Normal. Hold up your phone at "hashtag roast me". |
| 5 | Grin on "roast them". |
| 6 | Point at the camera on "you're". |
| 6b | (optional) Normal. |
| 7 | Smile, then laugh after the line and keep the camera rolling for 2 s. |

**App screens: I'll capture them myself** from the app (the sign-up page,
writing a #roastme post, a real roast), like I did for Reel 1. You only
record the talking clips.

**Before you post,** set your bio link to **yoru-social.vercel.app/join**
(straight to sign-up, not the home page).

## Then every night: "Roasting your posts, night N"

This replaces slot 3 of YORU Daily once people start posting (until then,
keep roasting yourself).

1. On YORU, open the **#roastme** tag. Pick 3 posts from people who have
   "Let others roast me" on.
2. Summon the Menace roast on each.
3. Film yourself reading each roast out loud and reacting, plus one opener
   and one closer.

| Clip | Say |
|---|---|
| open | "Night [N] of roasting your posts on my app. Three of you posted. Let's go." |
| for each | "This is from [their username]." Then read the roast and react for real. |
| close | "Want to be next? Hashtag roast me on YORU, link in my bio." |

Keep it fun, not mean:
- The roast's intensity follows what each person picked in their settings.
- Skip any post that's about someone's body, family or something
  personal, even if it's tagged.
- Never feature anyone who seems to have their account to the side of the
  rules (say they look younger than 13).

**Send me each night:** your 5 clips. I'll screen-capture the 3 posts and
roasts myself if you send me the post links, and put the username on
screen so they can share it, which brings in their friends too.
