# YORU Reel 3: "Still waiting for it to pop?"

## What the three references do

**The balloon (FYD Studios): the hook.** 7 seconds of a balloon, a needle
coming down, and a "FRAMES TO IMPACT 204 … 001" countdown. Nothing happens.
Then the voice says "You just watched a balloon for 7 seconds. A balloon
that doesn't exist. And you're still here, waiting for it to pop." A
"watching 7.7s" timer counts your own watch time at the top. It turns to the
brand ("Now imagine that was your brand"), and only pops at the very end,
so you stay for the pop. Why it works:
- **An unfinished event.** The brain hates an open loop (the balloon has to
  pop), so you keep watching.
- **A countdown.** Numbers going down promise a moment you don't want to
  miss.
- **It calls you out.** "You're still here" makes you notice you're
  hooked, which is surprising, so you keep going.
- **The payoff comes last.** The pop is the last thing, so people watch to
  the end, and the end loops back to the start.

**The motion-graphics one (Kreatively): the edit.**
- Words fade and sharpen in one at a time (blur → sharp, a slight scale).
- A fake app UI is being used: a cursor types in a search bar, a result
  appears, a "+" button grows into "Apply", then "Booked ✓".
- Clean glass cards, a checklist that ticks, smooth motion blur on every
  move.

**The talking head (Will Dowding): the structure.** "I'm not famous, I've
got
10 followers" honesty, a big "3" behind him for his 3 goals, words behind
him, B-roll cut in, ends on "let's see how this goes… follow". This is the
behind-me style we already use.

**Our version:** the balloon's hook, the motion-graphics edit, and the
behind-me words, all in YORU's own world. **The balloon is a heart, the
"like" heart from every other app.** It never pops until the end, and when
it pops it bursts into YORU's reactions: 🤡 💀 🧢 🔥. That's the brand in
one image: the like is dead, here's what replaces it.

## What to record (phone only)

**Part A: your voice over the heart (4 lines).** Use the **Voice Memos /
Recorder app**, phone about **15 cm from your mouth**, in a quiet room (a
closet full of clothes is perfect: no echo). Say it **calm and low, almost
like you're telling a secret**, with small pauses. **3 takes of each line.**

| Clip | Say |
|---|---|
| V1 | "You just watched a heart for seven seconds." |
| V2 | "A heart that doesn't even exist." |
| V3 | "And you're still here… waiting for it to pop." (pause before "waiting") |
| V4 | "Still waiting for it to pop?" (a little smile in your voice) |

**Part B: you on camera (5 lines).** Same as before: phone **sideways**,
front camera, **propped up about 1 metre away** at eye level, light in front
of you, wall behind you. Wait 1 s, say it, wait 1 s. **2 takes each.**

| Clip | Say | How |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | "That's every app. You wait for likes that don't mean anything." | Straight at the lens, a bit fed up |
| F2 | "So I'm seventeen, and I built my own." | Small proud smile on "built my own" |
| F3 | "It's called YORU." | Simple, confident. Say "YO-ru" clearly |
| F4 | "You don't heart a post. You clown it. Skull it. Call cap." | A beat between each one, point to the side (the cards appear there) |
| F5 | "Send this to the friend who's still waiting for likes." | Smile, then laugh and keep rolling 2 s |

That's everything. I make the heart, the needle, the countdown, the pop,
the search bar, the cards and the button myself.
