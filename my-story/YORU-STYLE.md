# YORU video style: LOCKED v2 (2026-11-04): the "unslop" rules

Every YORU video follows these rules, **always**. They come word for word
from the reference ("How to unslop your AI video edits") plus what's
visible in it. This replaces v1 (no more words behind you, no night
relight, no typewriter or italic captions, no kinetic text).

## The rules (from the reference, word for word)

1. **TikTok-style captions. Never italic serifs.**
2. **On-screen hooks:** no background, **or** a white background, **or** a
   black background. Nothing else.
3. **Real screen recordings and images, taken by you.** Never have me
   automate or scrape them. You take the pictures / screen recordings
   yourself, download them, and they go into a **reference folder** I use.
   (So I no longer capture app screens myself. You send them.)
4. **Never animate images.** No tilted overlay cards. Images go in
   **vertically straight, no round corners, no white outlines.**
5. **Cut out any dead space longer than 0.1 seconds.** **Plain cuts only.**
   No clip transitions.
6. **Motion animations only occasionally,** and **never the kinetic
   style** (no big animated flying words). When used: clean structural
   animations of objects (blocks, a folder, a phone coming apart) that
   explain something, on a dark background.
7. **No hundred editing styles, no crazy fonts.** The idea is what
   performs.
8. **End on a keyword CTA:** "comment the word ___ and I'll send it to
   you." (Only offer what you'll really send.)

## Exactly how it looks (measured from the reference)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Format** | Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, natural light, face centred, a mic in hand or a lav |
| **Font** | One font: Inter. No italics anywhere. |
| **Captions** | 1–3 words at a time, white, bold, a soft dark shadow, centred at **68% down** the frame. About 68 px. Time them exactly to the words. |
| **Caption accent** | At most one word per line: a short **coloured underline** under it (YORU red #E11D48), never a different font. |
| **Hook text** | Top of the frame (about 12–17% down), 2 lines, medium weight, about 60 px. Either white with a shadow (no background), black text on white boxes, or white text on black boxes, square-ish corners. Stays up during the opening line. |
| **Handle** | A small Instagram icon + @yourhandle, right side, about a quarter of the way down. |
| **Inserts** | **Split screen:** the screenshot / screen recording fills the **top half** on a near-black panel (#141010), **straight, square corners, no outline, not moving.** You stay in the bottom half and keep talking. The caption sits on the line between them. |
| **Motion graphic** (occasionally) | Full frame or top half, dark background, objects that build or come apart (isometric blocks, a folder filling, a phone layer by layer), labelled with small tags. |
| **Cuts** | Plain hard cuts. No zooms, flashes, whooshes or transitions. Every pause over 0.1 s removed. |
| **Sound** | Your voice, clean and loud. Optional quiet music bed or a trending sound added in the app. No sound effects on cuts. |
| **End** | No end card. The last line is the keyword CTA. |

## What I need from you each time
- The talking take(s), filmed **vertically**, phone propped at eye level.
- **Your own screenshots / screen recordings** for every insert the script
  asks for (phone screenshots, screen recordings, photos of your laptop
  screen). Send them as files; I keep them in that video's reference

- Your Instagram **@handle**, for the corner.
