# YORU video style: LOCKED (v1, 2026-11-04)

Every YORU video uses this. Same look, same sound, same ending, so people
recognise a YORU video in the first second, before they read anything.
Change it only on purpose, and write the change here.

**Slogan:** *the night is for everyone* (on the end card and in every
caption's sign-off).

## 1. Format
- **Landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps.** Phone sideways, front camera, propped up
  about 1 m away at eye level.
- **Length 15–30 s.** Under 20 s for 3 a.m. thoughts and POVs, up to 35 s
  for app videos.

## 2. The look (night)
- **Light:** room lights off, one light from the front (laptop screen at
  full brightness, just below the face). Never a light behind you.
- **Grade:** cool moonlit blue, the face lifted, the room darker than you.
  Strong noise reduction, then a light film grain (7%) and a vignette.
- **Camera:** a slow push-in (about 3–4% per line). No jumpy punch-ins.
  A soft flash only when the scene changes.

## 3. Words behind you
- **The 2–4 words that matter** in each line, placed in the open space
  beside you, tucked **behind your head** (cut-out masks).
- **Font:** Inter Black, capitals, two lines at most. Colour: white
  fading to moonlight blue (#B8C6FF) with a soft glow. They **sharpen out of
  a blur** (0.45 s).
- **Numbers always as digits:** "100 POSTS", "1 YEAR", "12 LIKES".

## 4. Captions
- **Typewriter:** DejaVu Sans Mono, white, bottom left, typing as you say
  it, with a block cursor. Key words in soft red (#FF8FA3).
- The closing line ("Now let that sink in", "Start it tonight") types big,
  centred, in capitals.

## 5. Graphics (only when the video shows the app)
- Glass cards, a cursor that taps, a search bar that types, a "+" that
  grows into a button and clicks ✓. Everything blurs in; nothing just
  appears.
- The app's real screens on a phone that slides in from the right.

## 6. Brand marks
- **A small red 夜** bottom right on every frame. Nothing else in the corners.
- **End card:** fade to black → 夜 (glowing red) → YORU → *the night is for
  everyone*. 2.5 s. Then it loops to the first frame.

## 7. Sound
- **Voice first.** Noise removal, warmth, presence, compression, de-ess,
  then the master at about −14 LUFS.
- **Under it:** a quiet low drone (A minor) or the night score, ducked
  under the voice. Soft piano notes into black pauses. Whooshes only on
  graphics.
- **A soft bell** when 夜 appears. Same sound every video: that's the
  audio logo.

## 8. Structure (every video)
1. **Frame 1 hooks without sound:** a POV or "if you…" line typed at the
   top, and **you look up into the lens** (or the lights snap off).
2. **By 3 s:** the promise ("this is for you", "here's 4 things").
3. **The middle:** one idea, said simply. A repeat or a number that grows.
4. **The turn to YORU** comes late and short. One line, never a pitch
   ("I built an app at night. It's called YORU.").
5. **The payoff is last**, then the end card, then the loop.
6. **Send prompt** in the caption: "Send this to the friend who…".

## 9. Captions under the post
- **First line** = the hook, word for word.
- **One or two lines** of story.
- **The send prompt.**
- *the night is for everyone 🌙*
- **3–5 hashtags:** #3amthoughts #motivation #buildinpublic
  #teenentrepreneur (+ one about the topic).

## 10. What I need from you each time
- **Talking takes:** each video in one go, 3 times, 1 s of silence at
  each end.
- **B-roll when the script asks for it,** 5–10 s each: laptop typing, the
  window at night, your hand on the light switch.
- **App screens:** I capture them myself.
